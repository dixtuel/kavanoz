    How Virtual Jar Works

    1. Shared Jar: Everyone leaves a note or memory in the same communal jar, either signed or anonymously.

    2. Shaking & Envelopes: Notes drop into the jar as cute sealed letters. You can drag envelopes, tap to open notes, or shake the jar.

    3. Shelves: Once a jar reaches its capacity, it is automatically shelved and archived, starting a fresh new jar.

    4. Time Capsule (Email): Optionally, choose a future date to receive your note delivered straight to your inbox.

    5. Management Key: Each note generates a private key so you can edit or delete your entry at any time.
