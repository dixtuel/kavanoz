Privacy Policy & Data Notice

This privacy notice explains how Virtual Jar processes and protects your personal data in full compliance with the European General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law ("KVKK" No. 6698), written in clear, honest, and accessible language.

1. Data Controller & Scope

The data controller for this service is Site Operator. Inquiries regarding privacy and data rights may be directed to: [email protected]

2. Public Board Notice

Virtual Jar's active jars function as publicly accessible boards. Any note and nickname dropped into a public jar can be read by anyone browsing the site. Please refrain from writing private secrets, government IDs, phone numbers, or confidential personal information in publicly visible notes.

3. What We Process & How We Protect It

We adhere to strict data minimization principles, processing only what is necessary to run the service:

  • Note Content & Display Name: Your note text is encrypted at rest using military-grade AES-256-GCM encryption in the database. The decryption key is isolated in the server runtime environment and never stored with the database.
  • Email Address (Optional): Collected only if you schedule future email delivery. It is similarly protected with AES-256-GCM encryption and never shared or made public.
  • Management Key (Zero-Knowledge): The key presented to you upon note creation is stored exclusively as a one-way salted cryptographic hash (SHA-256). We cannot view or reconstruct your plaintext key.
  • IP Address: Evaluated transiently at the edge for rate limiting and bot defense. It is never stored in the database alongside your note.

4. Self-Destructing Data Retention

We believe data should not linger indefinitely on servers:

  • Email Purging: Once your scheduled note is successfully delivered (or after 6 failed delivery attempts), your email address is permanently and irreversibly wiped from the database.
  • Expiring Notes: Notes created with an expiration date are automatically purged by background workers once their duration concludes.
  • Zero Marketing: Your email address is never used for marketing, newsletters, or advertising, nor is it sold or shared with any broker.

5. Direct, Tracker-Free Email Delivery

Scheduled emails are dispatched directly through our own self-hosted mail server (Postfix/SMTP). We do not use third-party mass marketing services (such as Mailchimp or SendGrid), and our emails never contain tracking pixels or click-tracking URL wrappers.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure

We utilize trusted infrastructure partners to provide edge performance, security, and spam prevention:

  • Turso (ChiselStrike libSQL): Distributed encrypted database engine.
  • hCaptcha (Intuition Machines, Inc.): Anti-bot challenge verification on submissions.
  • NVIDIA NIM Safety Model: Incoming messages are screened in real-time over TLS to catch illegal or abusive content; your text is never used to train models.
  • Cloudflare Pages & Edge: Global CDN distribution and layer-7 DDoS mitigation.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: Used only if you consent; cookie-free and stores no IP or personal data. See section 7.

7. Cookies & Client-Side Performance

We do not use tracking or behavioral profiling cookies:

  • Language Choice: Saved solely in your browser's local storage (localStorage).
  • Web Analytics Consent: The "Accept / Essential only" choice shown on your first visit is not a cookie — it's stored in your browser's localStorage (key cf_analytics_consent). Only choosing "Accept" loads Cloudflare Web Analytics, which itself uses no cookies and stores no IP address or personal data, producing only aggregated, anonymous traffic statistics. Reset anytime by clearing your browser data.
  • Battery & Low-Power Modes: Battery status and network constraints are computed dynamically via client-side web APIs; no hardware metrics are ever sent to our servers.

8. Your Rights (GDPR & KVKK)

Under GDPR (Arts. 12–21) and KVKK (Art. 11), you have the right to inspect, correct, and demand the erasure of personal data concerning you.

Direct Self-Service Control: Using your Management Key via the "Manage Note" screen, you can inspect, edit, or permanently erase your note and email address instantly with a single click. If you have misplaced your key, you can submit an erasure request to [email protected] by verifying the contents of your note.

9. Security Architecture

All client-server communications are secured using modern TLS encryption. Sensitive data fields are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, and server secrets remain strictly isolated.
