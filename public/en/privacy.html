Privacy Policy

This page explains what personal data Virtual Jar processes, why, and for how long, in line with Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and general good practice.

An important note first

Virtual Jar is a shared, public board. Any note you drop in the jar (and your display name, if you give one) is readable by anyone — no account needed, anyone who clicks can see it. Don't write anything in the jar you wouldn't want shared.

Data controller

The data controller for this site is its operator. Questions: your-email@example.com

What we collect

  • Note text and display name: stored encrypted on the server, but shown publicly by design — that's the point of the site. Leave the name blank and you appear as "Anonymous."
  • Your email address (fully optional): collected only if you want the note also emailed to you later. Stored encrypted, never shown to anyone, and permanently deleted the moment delivery succeeds (or finally fails).
  • Management key: shown to you once when you save a note — your only way to edit or delete it later. Only a one-way hash of it is stored on the server; if you lose it, we can't recover it either, since we never know the original key.
  • IP address: kept briefly in server logs solely for abuse prevention / rate limiting.

Purpose and legal basis

Publicly showing your note and display name is based on the explicit consent you give when submitting the form (KVKK Art. 5/1) — it's the core function of this site. Your email address, given through a separate explicit consent, is processed only to deliver the note to you on the date you chose.

Retention

If you choose "leave it to the site owner" when dropping a note, it stays up indefinitely as long as it doesn't violate moderation rules (you can always delete it yourself with your management key). If you pick a date, it's automatically and permanently deleted then. Your email address is deleted immediately once delivery succeeds, or finally fails after 6 attempts.

Third parties

  • hCaptcha (Intuition Machines, Inc.) — runs on submission to prevent bots/abuse.
  • NVIDIA NIM and a local wordlist filter — check submitted text for illegal/harmful content; your text is sent to this third party (NVIDIA) during that check.
  • Email delivery runs from our own mail server — no third-party email marketing service is used.

Your rights

You can find out whether your data is processed, request information, correction or deletion, and object to processing. Using your management key, you can permanently delete or edit your note (and its email address, if any) any time via "Manage your note." If you've lost your key, you can request manual deletion at your-email@example.com if you can verify which note is yours.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Note text and email addresses are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in the database; the encryption key is kept separately from the database, only in the server environment. Your management key is stored only as a one-way hash.
