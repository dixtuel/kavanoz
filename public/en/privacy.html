Privacy Policy

This page explains what personal data Virtual Jar processes, why, and for how long, in line with Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and general good practice.

Data controller

The data controller for this site is its operator. Questions: your-email@example.com

What we collect

  • Jar text: your note, stored encrypted (AES-256) on the server.
  • Your email address: collected only to deliver the jar, stored encrypted, and permanently deleted the moment delivery succeeds (or finally fails).
  • Display name: stored only if you provide one; nothing is collected if you leave it blank.
  • IP address: kept briefly in server logs solely for abuse prevention / rate limiting.

Purpose and legal basis

Based on the explicit consent you give when submitting the form, data is processed only to: (1) deliver your note by email on the date you chose, (2) show it on the public wall once opened, if you chose "public," (3) prevent abuse (spam, harassment).

Retention

Unconfirmed jars (unverified email) are automatically deleted within 48 hours. The email address of a delivered jar (or one that failed after 6 attempts) is deleted immediately. Text of "private" jars is not retained once its purpose is fulfilled; text of "public" jars remains visible on the wall after opening, since that's the visibility you chose upfront.

Third parties

  • hCaptcha (Intuition Machines, Inc.) — runs on submission to prevent bots/abuse.
  • NVIDIA NIM and a local wordlist filter — check submitted text for illegal/harmful content; your text is sent to this third party (NVIDIA) during that check.
  • Email delivery runs from our own mail server — no third-party email marketing service is used.

Your rights

You can find out whether your data is processed, request information, correction or deletion, and object to processing. You can permanently delete your jar and its data at any time via the link in your confirmation or delivery email. For anything else, contact your-email@example.com.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Your jar text and email address are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in the database; the encryption key is kept separately from the database, only in the server environment.
