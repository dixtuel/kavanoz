Accessibility Statement

Virtual Jar is an open time-capsule platform where users can leave notes for today or years into the future. We are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities, continually improving the experience for everyone.

Standards & Conformance

This website is designed and maintained to adhere to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1 Level AA) standards.

Accessibility Features

  • Semantic HTML5: Structured with native landmark elements (<header>, <main>, <footer>, <nav>) and proper heading levels for screen reader clarity.
  • Full Keyboard Accessibility: Creating notes, copying management keys, browsing archived shelves, and navigating modal dialogs can all be operated via keyboard (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space, and Escape).
  • High Contrast & Clean Typography: Colors and typography are calibrated for optimal readability across diverse light conditions and displays.
  • Physics Animations & Accessible Alternatives: While the interactive jar uses Matter.js physics for floating letters, all notes are equally accessible via keyboard-navigable side drawers and modal dialogs.
  • Responsive & Zoom Support: Fully responsive on mobile, tablet, and desktop screens, supporting up to 200% zoom without loss of functionality or overflow issues.
