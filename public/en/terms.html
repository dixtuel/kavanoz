Terms of Use

By using Virtual Jar, you agree to the following.

What this is

Virtual Jar is a free, account-free, shared and public board. Whatever you drop in (and your display name, if given) is readable by anyone. Once the jar reaches a certain number of notes, it moves to the "shelf" and a new jar opens; shelved jars stay public too.

Management key — lost means gone

When you drop a note, you're shown a management key once. It's the only way to edit or delete that note. Only a one-way hash of it is kept on the server — if you lose the key, no one, including us, can recover access to that note on your behalf. Keep it somewhere safe.

Optional email delivery

You don't have to provide an email address. If you do, your note is sent to that address on the date you choose (up to 5 years out). No confirmation email is sent to that address beforehand — delivery simply happens on the date you (or whoever left the note on your behalf) set. Delivery isn't guaranteed; issues on the recipient's mail server or an address becoming invalid can prevent it.

Prohibited content

Notes containing violence, sexual abuse, hate speech, harassment, incitement to illegal activity, or unauthorized sharing of someone else's personal data are not accepted and are rejected by automated moderation. The site owner may also remove a note that violates these rules, even without the management key.

Limitation of liability

The service is provided "as is." We are not liable for the accuracy of published content, loss of access due to a lost management key, delays in delivery timing, or outages of third-party services (mail server, hCaptcha, content moderation API).
