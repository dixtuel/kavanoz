Terms of Use

Virtual Jar is a free, account-free digital time capsule and shared board for memories, letters, and wishes. By using this service, you agree to the following terms.

1. Nature of the Service

Virtual Jar is designed with two core modes of interaction:

  • Public Jars (Shared Wall): When you drop a note into the active jar, your message and chosen display name are displayed publicly on the wall for everyone to read. Once a jar reaches full capacity, it is moved to the "shelf" and a new jar opens. Archived shelf jars remain permanently public.
  • Letters to the Future (Time-Locked Delivery): When you specify a future date, your note remains sealed until that date arrives, at which point it is automatically and securely delivered to your optional recipient email address.

2. Zero-Knowledge Management Key

When you create a note, our system generates a unique Management Key displayed to you only once:

  • This key is the exclusive and authoritative way to edit or permanently delete your note at any time.
  • Our servers never store your plaintext key; only a salted one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256) is kept.
  • Important Notice: Because this architecture is strictly zero-knowledge, if you lose your key, nobody—including site operators—can access, edit, or recover the note on your behalf. Please store your key safely.

3. Optional Email Delivery

Providing an email address is entirely optional:

  • If provided, your note will be dispatched directly to that email on your designated target date (up to 5 years into the future).
  • No advance confirmation or promotional emails will ever be sent to this address; transmission triggers strictly on the target date.
  • Your email address is permanently purged from the database the moment delivery succeeds (or after 6 failed retry attempts).

4. Acceptable Content & Moderation

Virtual Jar is dedicated to warm, thoughtful, and constructive sharing. The following content is strictly prohibited:

  • Violence, threats, harassment, hate speech, or discrimination,
  • Sexually explicit, pornographic, or abusive material,
  • Promotion of illegal substances, crime, or fraud,
  • Unauthorized sharing or doxxing of another individual's personal information (national IDs, phone numbers, private addresses, etc.).

Every note is screened upon submission by local word filters and the NVIDIA NIM AI safety model. Notes violating community standards are rejected immediately. The operator reserves the right to remove any content violating applicable laws or safety guidelines.

Virtual Jar is an open-source, non-commercial community project provided "AS-IS" without warranty of any kind. We cannot be held liable for third-party user content, loss of access resulting from misplaced management keys, or delays caused by third-party mail providers or edge network outages.
