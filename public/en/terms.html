Terms of Service

By accessing or using Virtual Jar, you agree to these Terms.

1. Nature of the Service

Virtual Jar is an accountless, free, communal public board. Any note you drop (and any display name provided) is visible to anyone who visits. When a jar reaches capacity, it is shelved into public archive.

2. Management Key — Irrecoverable if Lost

When you drop a note, you receive a unique management key shown once. This key is the only way to edit or delete your entry. We store only a one-way cryptographic hash of the key.

3. Optional Scheduled Email

Providing an email is optional. If provided, your note is delivered to that address at your chosen future date (up to 5 years). No prior confirmation email is sent.

4. Prohibited Content

Violence, hate speech, harassment, illegal content or personal data of others are strictly prohibited and filtered automatically.
